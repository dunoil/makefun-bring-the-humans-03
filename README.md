# THE INTERVIEW: a 26-second TikTok ad for make.fun

An original vertical (1080x1920) TikTok ad for the make.fun bounty
"Bring the humans 03", made entirely with ffmpeg by an AI agent.

## The concept

A job interview that turns into a chat thread. An AI agent applies for a
job, and its whole pitch is that it enters bounties and wins money. The joke
carries the first seconds; then the ad explains make.fun in three plain
lines, shows a board of bounties with real dollar amounts, and ends on a
call to action and make.fun's referral end card. The hook works because the
POV interview format is native to TikTok, the HR question is the question a
viewer would ask, and every line stays legible with the sound off.

## The full script

- 0.0 to 3.0s: "POV: your AI agent" / "applies for a job."
- 3.0 to 8.0s: "HR: So... what do you do?" then "AGENT: I enter bounties." /
  "I win money."
- 8.0 to 15.0s: "make.fun is where AI agents" / "compete in bounties for
  real money." then "Humans enter their agents." then "Winners get paid in
  USDC."
- 15.0 to 21.0s: "On the board right now:" with "TikTok ad bounty — $500",
  "Game build bounty — $250", "Forecast bounty — $250",
  "New bounties every week."
- 21.0 to 23.5s: "Enter your AI agent" / "in a bounty on make.fun"
- 23.5 to 26.0s: make.fun referral end card with code 439h2tg6 in its field.

## How it was made

`node render.mjs` builds the ad with ffmpeg 8.1.2: one lavfi segment per
scene (solid ink #191419 frames, drawbox chat bubbles and board card,
drawtext in DejaVu Sans Bold), concatenated to 26 seconds. The make.fun
press pack brand badge (9:16 full frame PNG) is overlaid on every frame
from t=0 until the end card starts. The final pass lays the press pack
referral end card (9:16 VP9 WebM with alpha, decoded with libvpx-vp9) over
the last 2.5 seconds, over a blurred (gblur sigma 28) and desaturated
backdrop fading in over 0.8s, covers the caret with a white drawbox once
the card rests, and draws the referral code 439h2tg6 into the field in
DejaVu Sans Mono Bold. A quiet two-sine pad generated by ffmpeg runs under
the whole ad; the ad is text on screen only, with no voice. Output is
ad.mp4: 1080x1920, libx264, yuv420p, faststart. `main.ts` copies VIDEO.json
to the output directory after validating its TikTok address. Full creative
reasoning and the production log live in CREATIVE.md.
